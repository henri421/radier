Suite FR

Raft

Raft of any outline, with openings, on Winkler springs with uplift, under columns and walls: settlements, ground pressure, Wood-Armer design moments (EN 1997-3, 5.2.4.2, 5.5.6 and Annex B), to the 2004 or the 2025 Eurocode 7.

Not covered: coupled soil (Boussinesq), piled raft, variable thickness, reinforcement and punching (EC2 and Punching tools), dynamic effects, swelling.

Raft

Outline: points “x; y” separated by “/”, inside the Lx × Ly rectangle; empty: the whole rectangle. Openings separated by “/”: “x1; y1; x2; y2” for a rectangle, or a list of points. A cell counts if its centre lies on the raft.

Rectangular thin plate finite cells (ACM, 12 degrees of freedom). A mesh close to the column size is enough, with at most 20 thousand nodes.
